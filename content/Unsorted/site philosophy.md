The part of the website where I talk about the website

This site was built using quartz and is hosted on github pages. All for free (minus days of banging my head against my keyboards)

## Why?

Really enjoyed hand-making a home page as a part of [CS50X](https://cs50.harvard.edu/x/) and since I already take notes in Obsidian, I decided to use that aesthetic as a starting point :D

I've made this site to serve as a map of my digital footprint

I don't like the idea of having my entire internet presence being tied to an Instragram or X account cause those are all just other websites at the end of the day

I've also learned that clear writing is a prerequisite to clear thinking so this will be my platform to practice that
